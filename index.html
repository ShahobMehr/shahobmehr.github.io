About

Shahob Mehr

Based in Los Angeles. M.A. in Sports Management.

Shahob Mehr builds and monetizes audiences for athletes, sports media personalities and consumer brands.

As Director of Content Strategy and Partnership Management at William Raymond Communications, where he was the first hire, he leads a team of four overseeing 40+ accounts across the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and international soccer.

His programs have generated $144K+ in direct platform revenue, $80K+ in negotiated brand deals and 20M+ campaign views, including launching Ian Rapoport as the first NFL journalist on TikTok and growing him into a LinkedIn Top Voice.

He works directly with league offices, including the NFL, NHL, MLB and the English Premier League, as well as brand partners such as American Airlines, Toyota and Taco Bell.
